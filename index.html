07 — FAQ

Things people actually ask.

Anything missing? Email [email protected] — a real editor on our team answers within a day.

Is the AI footage commercially usable? +

Yes — every generation through Reelyard is wrapped in a commercial license, indemnified up to $50k per project on Studio plans. We only ship models whose providers allow this.

Will my Premiere project break if I uninstall? +

No. Anything Reelyard places in your timeline becomes a regular, editable asset on disk. The panel is just a vending machine; what you cut is yours.

How do payouts work for sellers? +

Weekly via Stripe in 47 countries. You see every sale, refund, and country breakdown. We take 15% net of payment fees — no hidden “feature” surcharges.

Can I use Reelyard on iPad / mobile? +

The marketplace and AI tools are fully responsive — you can prompt, preview and queue renders from your phone. Editing extensions are desktop-only (Premiere, Resolve).

Do credits roll over? +

On Studio and Studio Team, yes — up to 2× your monthly allowance. Starter credits reset each month.
